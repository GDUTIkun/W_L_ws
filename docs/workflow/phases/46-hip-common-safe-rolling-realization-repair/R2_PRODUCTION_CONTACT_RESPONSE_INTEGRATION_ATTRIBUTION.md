## Production equation inventory and first missing relation

The production QP has 42 decision variables:

```text
x = [nudot(12), tau(6), aggregate wrench W(12), interaction slack(12)].
```

It hard-enforces reduced dynamics, torque limits, the 37-row-per-side aggregate wrench cone and
acceleration limits:

```text

```

Contact acceleration, rolling/xi and interaction-wrench relations are objectives (the last with explicit
slack), not a plant contact constitutive law. Corrected R1 restricts the wrench representation but does not
determine the reaction generated by the plant. Therefore production already binds `W` to `tau/nudot`
through force balance, but does not bind it to the reaction produced by the same `tau`, state and contact
regime. The first missing production relation is a controller-side constitutive/complementarity
contact-response law.

## Diagnostic affine-law provenance

The diagnostic law is derived at fixed state and fixed MuJoCo active set from:

```text
M qacc = qfrc_smooth + B tau + J^T f
f = D (aref - J qacc)
```

Eliminating `qacc` and the solver-row reaction gives:

```text
qacc = (M + J^T D J)^-1 (q_smooth + B tau + J^T D aref)
Qc(tau) = Qc0 + Qct tau.
```

This is `R1`: an eliminated closed-response operator. It is neither an `R0` primitive constitutive law nor
an empirical `R2` fit. Its coefficients require MuJoCo `efc_J`, `efc_D`, `efc_aref`, selected equality and
contact rows, the current pyramidal friction active set, and the solver/contact parameters that generated
them. It does not require a post-solve reaction or future `qacc`, but those MuJoCo runtime internals make it
`P2`, not production-computable `P0/P1`.

Its exactness is local to the frozen state, contact topology, equality/contact active rows, pyramidal
friction regime and `solref/solimp`-dependent solver response. The corrected directional probes establish
trust only inside that frozen regime; they do not establish a global or deployable production law.

## Double-counting and insertion audit

There is no literal force double-counting when the affine map and the explicit dynamics use exactly the
same `M/B/J/D/aref` and active set: substituting the primitive contact law into dynamics and then Schur
eliminating it yields the same coupled equations. In that narrow algebraic domain, adding the condensed
reaction equality while retaining acceleration is an equivalent consistency formulation.

That observation does not legalize a production hard equality. Current production does not own the
MuJoCo primitive law or its solver state, so appending the simulator-derived closed response would import
a second, simulator-specific full-dynamics model and can overconstrain a nonidentical production model.
Replacing or softly tracking the relation would have the same missing-provenance problem. The preferred
form is therefore `I4` (no insertion).

Per the strict stop order, the `P2` result stops the audit before production rank augmentation, physical-H0
witness, shadow-QP, independent prediction, or before/after contact-response residual claims. The already
frozen corrected diagnostic Stage-R PASS is evidence that the diagnostic algebra is internally consistent;
it is not a shadow production-QP authorization.
